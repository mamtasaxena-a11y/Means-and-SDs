Statistical Test Decision Matrix

Statistical test choice depends directly on the measurement level of your Independent Variable (IV) and Dependent Variable (DV).

Master Test Selection Decision Guide
Independent Variable (IV) Dependent Variable (DV) Research Goal Appropriate Statistical Test
Categorical (2 Independent Groups) Continuous Compare 2 group means Independent Samples t-Test
Categorical (2 Related / Repeated Measures) Continuous Compare Pre vs Post scores Paired Samples t-Test
Categorical (3+ Independent Groups) Continuous Compare 3+ group means One-Way ANOVA (F-Test)
Continuous (Numeric scale) Continuous (Numeric scale) Assess linear strength & direction Pearson Correlation (r)
Continuous or Categorical (1 or more predictors) Continuous Predict DV value or fit line ($\hat{Y} = b_0 + b_1 X$) Simple / Multiple Linear Regression

t-Tests (t-Statistic)

When to use: Comparing means between 2 categorical groups.

Independent t-test: Unrelated groups (e.g., Treatment group vs Control group).

Paired t-test: Same participants measured twice (e.g., Weight before and after diet).

One-Way ANOVA (F-Test)

When to use: Comparing means across 3 or more categorical groups.

Why not multiple t-tests? Running multiple t-tests inflates Type I Error rate (α inflation). ANOVA evaluates variance between group means vs variance within groups in a single test.

Pearson Correlation (r)

When to use: Evaluating the linear relationship between two continuous variables.

Ranges from $-1.00$ (perfect negative correlation) to $+1.00$ (perfect positive correlation). $r = 0$ indicates no linear association.

Linear Regression

When to use: Modeling an outcome variable ($\hat{Y}$) based on one or more continuous or categorical predictor variables ($X$).

Provides line slope ($b_1$), intercept ($b_0$), and $R^2$ (proportion of variance explained).

Scenario 1: Exercise Program & Blood Pressure

A researcher tests whether a 12-week aerobic exercise program alters resting systolic blood pressure. 30 participants have their blood pressure measured immediately before beginning the program and again after completing the 12 weeks.

Independent Variable (IV): Time Point (2 repeated measures: Pre vs Post) -> Categorical

Dependent Variable (DV): Blood Pressure (mmHg) -> Continuous

Correct Statistical Choice: Paired Samples t-Test
